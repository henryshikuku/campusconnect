Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for new students.

Do I have to pay to join a club?

No. All clubs listed on CampusConnect are free to join for registered students.

Can first-year students attend events?

Yes. Most events are open to everyone, and some are made especially for new students.

Does the form on this site send my details?

No. The form is a practice form only, so your information is not sent or stored.

Where can I find more study help?
